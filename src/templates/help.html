Home (Product List)

The Home page displays a list of all products available at H&M, including their categories. This page helps you explore the full range of H&M products in one place.

  • View Products: Browse through all H&M products, with each product card showing the product name and category.
  • Search Products: Use the search bar at the top to find products by name or category. For example, type "shirt" to see all shirts available.
  • Filter by Store: Use the store filter dropdown to view products from H&M (this page only shows H&M products, so other store selections will show no results).
  • View Product Images: Click the "Show Image" button on any product card to view an image of the product. Click "Back to Product List" to return to the list.

Trending Products

The Trending Products page shows the top 15 products with the highest sales across all stores (H&M, Retail Store 1, and Retail Store 2) since January 2023. This helps you identify the most popular products across all stores.

  • View Trending Products: See the top 15 products ranked by total sales, with each card displaying the product name and the store it belongs to.
  • Search Products: Use the search bar to filter the list by product name. For example, search for "jeans" to see if any jeans are trending.
  • Filter by Store: Use the store filter dropdown to view trending products from a specific store (H&M, Retail Store 1, or Retail Store 2), or select "All Stores" to see the full list.
  • View Product Images: Click the "Show Image" button on a product card to see its image. Click "Back to Trending Products" to return to the list.

Top-Selling Products

The Top-Selling Products page highlights the top 5 best-selling products from Retail Store 1 and Retail Store 2 since January 2023. This page is useful for identifying high-performing products in these specific stores.

  • View Top-Selling Products: See the top 5 products from each store (Retail Store 1 and Retail Store 2), with each card showing the product name and store.
  • Search Products: Use the search bar to filter the list by product name. For example, search for "jacket" to see if any jackets are top-sellers.
  • Filter by Store: Use the store filter dropdown to view top-sellers from Retail Store 1 or Retail Store 2. Selecting "H&M" will show no results since this page only includes Retail Store 1 and Retail Store 2 products.
  • View Product Images: Click the "Show Image" button on a product card to view its image. Click "Back to Recommend products" to return to the list.

All Products

The All Products page provides a comprehensive list of products from all stores (H&M, Retail Store 1, and Retail Store 2), including their categories. This page is ideal for browsing the entire product catalog across all stores.

  • View All Products: Browse products from H&M, Retail Store 1, and Retail Store 2, with each card showing the product name, category, and store.
  • Search Products: Use the search bar to filter products by name or category. For example, search for "dress" to see all dresses across stores.
  • Filter by Store: Use the store filter dropdown to view products from a specific store (H&M, Retail Store 1, or Retail Store 2), or select "All Stores" to see the full list.
  • View Product Images: Click the "Show Image" button on a product card to view its image. Click "Back to Product List" to return to the list.

Lowest-Selling Products

The Lowest-Selling Products page lists the 5 products with the lowest sales from H&M since January 2023. This page helps identify underperforming products that may need attention.

  • View Lowest-Selling Products: See the 5 products with the lowest sales from H&M, with each card showing the product name and store (H&M).
  • Search Products: Use the search bar to filter the list by product name. For example, search for "scarf" to see if any scarves are underperforming.
  • Filter by Store: Use the store filter dropdown to view products from H&M (other store selections will show no results since this page only includes H&M products).
  • View Product Images: Click the "Show Image" button on a product card to view its image. Click "Back to Lowest-Selling Products" to return to the list.

Help

You're currently on the Help page! This page provides a detailed guide to navigating and using the Product Dashboard. Here are some general tips for using the dashboard:

  • Navigation: Use the sidebar on the left to navigate between different sections of the dashboard. On mobile devices, click the menu icon in the header to toggle the sidebar.
  • Dark Mode: Toggle between light and dark themes using the moon icon in the header for a more comfortable viewing experience.
  • Filtering and Searching: Most pages include a search bar and store filter dropdown at the top. Use these to quickly find products by name or filter by store.
  • Product Images: Click "Show Image" on any product card to view a product image. Use the "Back" button to return to the product list.
